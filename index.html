Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Applies to MyIslamicLife: Namaz Tracker (package com.thumbikr.MyIslamicLife) version 1.13 and later

1. Overview

MyIslamicLife (“the App”) helps you keep your daily worship in one place: prayer times and reminders, a five-prayer tracker, Quran reading goals, Qibla compass, Tasbih, Adhkar, Ramadan and fasting, Islamic days, donation records and a Zakat calculator. The App is made by IKR (“we”, “us”).

In short: your records stay on your phone. We do not run a server and we never receive your prayer records, Quran reading, fasts, donations or Zakat values. Data leaves your phone only for features you choose to use, and for the Google AdMob banner ads that keep the App free. We do not sell your data.

2. Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is saved only in the App's private storage on your phone. This includes:

  • Prayer records (on time, in jamaat, qaza, missed), qaza counts and extra prayers (Witr, Sunnah, Duha, Taraweeh)
  • Quran reading goals, reading time, “where I stopped” and khatam progress
  • Fasts, excused days and the optional private “period day” switch in women's mode
  • Donation records and the values you type into the Zakat calculator
  • Goals, streaks, badges, your chosen city or calculation method, prayer-time adjustments, alarms and other settings
  • If you sign in with Google for backup: your Google name and e-mail address, shown so you know which account is connected

We cannot see or access any of this data.

3. Features that use the internet or other services

FeatureWhat happensWho receives data
Google sign-in & Drive backup (optional) Saves and restores one backup file (MyIslamicLife-backup.json) in your own Google Drive, automatically (daily, weekly or monthly) or when you tap backup. Google (your account). Name, e-mail and the drive.file permission only.

Google AdMob: advertising ID, approximate location (from IP address), app interactions, crash logs and diagnostics. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
Reminders & alarms Prayer notifications and namaz, Sehri, Iftar and Tahajjud alarms are scheduled on your phone. No one.

All data sent over the internet is encrypted (HTTPS).

4. Permissions the App uses

  • Internet and network state – for ads, Google Drive backup and the mosque map.
  • Approximate location (optional) – for prayer times, Qibla direction and “Mosques near me”. You can choose a city instead.
  • Notifications – for prayer-time notifications and reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) and full-screen alarm – so the alarms you set ring on time and show over the lock screen with Stop and Snooze.
  • Vibration – for alarms and to tell you when you face the Qibla.
  • Advertising ID – used by Google AdMob for ads, measurement and fraud prevention.

The App does not use your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

5. How information is used

Information is used only to run the App's features: prayer times, tracking, statistics, reminders and alarms, Qibla, the mosque map, backup and restore, and showing ads. Your worship, fasting, donation and Zakat records are never used for advertising. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

6. Google user data (Limited Use disclosure)

MyIslamicLife's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google name, e-mail address and Drive access are used only to show the signed-in account and to back up and restore your own data. With the drive.file permission the App can see only files it created itself – not your other Drive files. This data is not transferred to others, not used for advertising and not read by people.

7. Your choices and controls

  • Use the App without an account – Google sign-in is optional.
  • Turn location off and pick a city instead.
  • Turn any feature off in More › Advanced features; turn notifications and alarms on or off in the App or in Android settings.
  • Turn automatic backup on or off, or sign out to stop Drive backup. You can also remove the App's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Use More › Erase all data, clear the App's data or uninstall the App to remove it. Backup files in your Google Drive or other storage stay until you delete them yourself (for example MyIslamicLife-backup.json in Drive). We have nothing to delete on our side because we never receive your data.

9. Security

Your records are kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Google Drive backup uses HTTPS. No method of storage is completely secure, so please keep a backup of your data.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The App does not ask for your age.

11. International transfers

Google and OpenStreetMap may process data outside your country under their own privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. Important changes may also be mentioned in the App's release notes.
